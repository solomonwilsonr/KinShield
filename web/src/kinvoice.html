FAQs

Honest answers about what KinVoice does, and what it doesn’t.

Does KinVoice detect fake or cloned voices?expand_more

No, on purpose. Voice-clone detection is an arms race that gets harder as clones improve. KinVoice reads what a call is asking someone to believe and do, so it works even against a perfect clone.

Do you have paying customers?expand_more

No. KinShield is pre-launch. The demo is real and live on AWS, but the product is not for sale and we are not claiming any users.

Is the location check real?expand_more

It's simulated in this demo. It compares a caller's stated location against a device the real person chose to share. It never tracks the caller. It is illustrative, not a shipped feature.

What about real phone calls?expand_more

The judge-reachable demo runs on scripted transcripts. Live-call handling (Amazon Chime SDK + Amazon Transcribe) is architecturally scoped and on the roadmap, not yet the shipped path.

What data do you store?expand_more

The call demo stores per-session risk events in DynamoDB and needs no sign-up. There's no phone number or contact list involved. In KinBot, messages are checked and forgotten. Signing in is optional. If you do, Kip saves the verdict and headline of each check (never the message itself) so you can see your history.

Is this medical, legal, or financial advice?expand_more

No. It's a fraud-signal tool and an educational demo. If you think you've been scammed, contact your bank and report to the FTC (reportfraud.ftc.gov) or FBI IC3 (ic3.gov).
