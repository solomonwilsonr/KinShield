How it works

Four steps, a few seconds

1

You share it

Paste a text or email (up to 2,000 characters), add a screenshot, or share a voicemail. For screenshots, a vision model on Amazon Bedrock (Qwen3-VL) reads the words and looks at logos, links and QR codes. For voicemails, a speech model on Bedrock (Mistral Voxtral) writes down what was said; the audio isn't stored.

2

Kip quotes the warning signs

KinShield's evidence detector (gpt-oss-20b on Amazon Bedrock) looks for seven fixed scam signs. It must quote the exact words, and KinBot drops any quote that isn't really in your message.

3

Kip investigates the links

After every verdict, an agent (gpt-oss-20b with tools) checks how old a website is and whether it imitates a brand, looks it up in phishing feeds, opens the link safely as a plain static fetch with no browser, checks phone numbers, and pulls FTC-based advice.

4

You get a next step, then ask anything

Plain steps, like calling back on a number you already have. Then ask follow-ups, like "but it came from his real number?"

What Kip looks for

The seven warning signs

The same fixed list KinShield uses on phone calls. Kip can't invent new ones, and every sign it reports comes with the words that raised it.

  • Pretending to be someoneGrandma, it's me.
  • Sudden emergencyI'm at the police station.
  • Asks for secrecyPlease don't tell Mom.
  • Asks for moneyI need $2,000 today.
  • Unusual paymentPay with gift cards or Bitcoin.
  • Official-sounding pressureThis is Officer Daniels with Social Security.
  • Rushing youYour account locks in 30 minutes.
Privacy and honest limits

What KinBot is, and what it isn't

lock

Your message isn't kept

Your message, screenshot or voicemail is checked and forgotten, and the conversation lives only in your browser tab. Signing in is optional. If you do, Kip saves the verdict and headline of each check (never the message itself) so you can see your history.

verified_user

It can be wrong

KinBot is an AI demo. A LOW result doesn't prove a message is safe. If money is involved, call back on a number you already trust.

science

KinModel is experimental

KinModel, our tiny keyword model, adds a second opinion. It's clearly labelled, and Kip's cited answer always comes first.

chat

It can't see your phone

Kip only reads what you share with it. It never sees your calls, contacts or accounts, and it will never ask for a password or code.

FAQs

Honest answers about what KinBot does, and what it doesn't.

Is KinBot free?expand_more

Yes. No sign-up is needed. Signing in is optional. If you do, Kip saves the verdict and headline of each check (never the message itself) so you can see your history. KinShield is pre-launch, with no paying customers.

What can I paste?expand_more

Text up to 2,000 characters (a text message, an email, or what a caller said), a screenshot, or a voicemail recording. Remove names and account details first.

Which AI model does it use?expand_more

All on Amazon Bedrock. The evidence detector is the same one as the KinVoice call demo: OpenAI's open-weight gpt-oss-20b with a structured prompt and server-side checks. Screenshots are read by Qwen3-VL, voicemails are written down by Mistral Voxtral, and the link investigator is gpt-oss-20b with tools. The KinModel second opinion is a small TF-IDF + logistic-regression classifier that runs inside the same AWS Lambda.

Does Kip open the links?expand_more

Only safely. The investigator fetches the page from AWS as plain static text (no browser, no scripts, no cookies, no forms), strips tracking codes from the link, and only keeps features like the page title and forms, never the page itself. It also checks the domain's age, brand look-alikes and public phishing feeds.

What if I already sent money?expand_more

Call your bank or payment provider now, using its official app or the number on your card, and report it at reportfraud.ftc.gov or ic3.gov. Tell KinBot "I already sent money" for the full checklist.
